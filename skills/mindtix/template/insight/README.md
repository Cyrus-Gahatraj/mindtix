# insight

The AI's evidence-based read of you. Written **only** by `/mindtix reflect`, from everything
else in the brain. Every claim carries evidence and a label.

- `knowledge.md`: topic levels 0-5. The learning skills use them to pitch difficulty
- `learning.md`: how you learn, measured from your sessions and reviews (which teaching
  formats stick, how well your confidence matches your answers, your mix of mistakes), ending in
  the Teaching plan that `/mindtix learn` follows
- `mind.md`, `habits.md`, `writing-style.md`, `connections.md`
- `scores.json`: numbers behind charts. Each result says how it was got in `method`: `asked`
  (you answered the test here), `self` (you wrote or stated it) or `inferred` (the AI's estimate)
