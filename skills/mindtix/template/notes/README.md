# notes

What you write on the site (Write and quick capture on Today) lands here. Unlike `raw/`, the
AI may tidy these: `/mindtix reflect` gives each note a title and `[[links]]`, merges
duplicates and groups them into subfolders, always keeping your words, then reads them for
`insight/`. `/mindtix capture` may file what they teach into `knowledge/`, and leaves them here.
